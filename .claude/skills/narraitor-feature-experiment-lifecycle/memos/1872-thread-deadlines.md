## Decision

- **HOLD** (round 6, held again in round 7). The declared rule says a split on G-B is a hold naming the cell: Crystal Lake in round 6, Harrowgate in round 7. PR #1878 stays a draft against develop; the round-5 clock stays shipped as it is, flag on.
- Failing gate, round 6: G-B in Crystal Lake, last-block Momentum 2 against a first-treated-block 3 (round 5 was 1 against 4). The blind judge's sharpest problem is the issue's own symptom, unchanged in kind: "every turn from 16 onward introduces a new offstage threat sound ... and none of them ever arrives". Harrowgate's G-A pass is a letter pass: its one resolution is the six-week vote recorded as resolved on being "formally called" at t29, and that judge's sharpest problem is that the vote is called seven times "and never once resolves".
- What round 6 did move, and why this is a hold and not a retire: every ledger metric went the right way in both cells (resolved 0 -> 1 and 0 -> 2, dropped 2 -> 0 and 1 -> 0, longest overdue streak 25 -> 7 on the one event-shaped thread the DUE NOW pick was aimed at, no near dues, no advance recorded as a bare reminder), and last-block Momentum is one point above round 5 in both cells (2 -> 3, 1 -> 2). The mechanism lands event-shaped threads (a sound at the shed came through inside four DUE NOW turns) and does nothing for state-shaped ones ("owes a debt" sat DUE NOW for 24 turns and was hand-waved). N=1 per cell, so a one-point move is inside the noise either way.
- **Round 7 (2026-08-18, `f213e706`): still HOLD, and the wording lever is spent.** The three re-entry changes were made as declared (OPEN files offstage threats and uncovered major events as event-shaped threads, RESOLVE must name the outcome, DUE NOW's landing list gains "the debt is collected", the empty-ledger move is owed) and the same two cells re-run. Harrowgate: G-A passes for real this time (the vote landed at t8 under DUE NOW with "voted to accept The Abacus Group's offer", 2 resolved / 0 dropped), and G-B fails hard (block 3 Momentum 1 against block 1's 3; round 6 was 3 vs 3). Crystal Lake: the seed filed one thread at t16 ("whatever caused the clang at Cabin Seven will make its presence known", due 18), it went DUE NOW at t21, the masked man arrived at t21 and killed the player by t24, and the session ended with an epilogue before the declared 31-45 block, so G-B is not scorable there and G-A fails on the count because the extractor filed the whole landing as advances and never wrote `resolved`. Blind judge on the shortened transcript: treated block Momentum 3, Stakes 4, everything else 2. A second Crystal Lake attempt was not run: Harrowgate fails G-B on its own, so the verdict is HOLD whichever way a 45-turn read came out; that is a decision, not a finding, and the cell stays unmeasured on this wording.
- What round 7 taught, and why the next turn is not wording: (1) telling the extractor to file every offstage threat multiplied the ledger (4 threads to 9, 6 overdue at t30) and most of the new threads were the same favor coming due under a new name: the seed's "those who appointed you will seek repayment" was DUE NOW from t9 to t30 while Henderson (t9), Sarah (t18) and Abernathy (t21) each arrived to collect and were filed as new threads instead of advancing or resolving the one already owed. (2) With six overdue actors in the block, "it has already been announced, do not deliver it as fresh news" did not hold: the judge counts Henderson's plea as news at t9/15/19/24/29 and the county man's arrival at t17/25/27, the last one pasted twice in one passage. (3) The stricter RESOLVE cut the round-6 fake resolution and also made the extractor withhold `resolved` on a real payoff (Crystal Lake's masked man). (4) DUE NOW does land event-shaped threads quickly when there is one to land: the vote in 3 turns with an outcome, the masked man in 1. Rounds 6 and 7 both moved the ledger and neither moved the last block.
- Re-entry condition (what flips this to ship): a round on the same two cells where the last-block Momentum is at or above the first treated block in BOTH cells and each session's resolutions name an outcome. Do not run an eighth wording round to get there. The two structural changes to build first, in order:
  1. Reconciliation on OPEN: before the extractor may open a thread, the arrival, message or move it wants to file is matched against the open threads (the DUE NOW one first) and becomes an advance or a resolution of that thread when it is the same pressure landing; a new thread only when nothing open covers it. That is what would have turned Harrowgate's nine threads into three and cleared the DUE NOW pick at t9.
  2. Something structural against re-announcement, not a sentence in the block: a thread that has fired once (its actor has arrived, its news has been delivered) is marked so in the ledger and rendered to the prompt as "already in the room" or is resolved, and a landing that the extractor records as an advance on a DUE NOW thread clears the pick for the next segment. Pair it with a check the harness can run: count the judge's "delivered as news again" instances per session and require zero for a ship.
  Then the same two cells again, and if Crystal Lake ends early again, a second attempt is part of the round, not optional.
- Not a retire: the direction is right and cheap (no new call, ~195 tokens while a pick exists), and round 7's Harrowgate vote and Crystal Lake masked man are the two cleanest deadline payoffs the campaign has recorded. What does not work is asking the wording to do the reconciliation the store should do; the flag and the seam still do not change.
