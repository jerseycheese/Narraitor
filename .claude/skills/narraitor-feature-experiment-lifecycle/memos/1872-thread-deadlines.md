- Decision point, not a tenth round by default. Four rounds on this PR have moved every ledger metric and moved a last block once, in one cell, unrepeated; wording (round 7) and two structural changes (rounds 8, 9) each fixed the loop the previous round named and exposed the next. The read that survives all four rounds: the prompt is being asked to narrate a mechanism that does not exist.
- **Why a tenth prompt round is the wrong instrument, checked after round 9 (filed as #1882).** Nothing in the story path can take anything from the character, so a landed thread has nothing to do. Crystal Lake's 7 inventory losses across 45 turns are all the player spending an item to brace a wall; the creature took nothing. Harrowgate's 12 failures cost zero items and changed no material state, and 11 of the 12 use the same "the words would not come" shape, on a branch that includes #1844 (the #1821 fix). `status.health` and `status.conditions` are declared on the character, set at creation, and never written anywhere in `src/`. The recordable channels that do work (inventory, NPC relationships, location) are never used by an arrived threat. This is why every round moved the ledger and not the story.
- Order of work, revised: #1882 first (a landed thread must impose a recordable consequence: an inventory item, an NPC's disposition, the player's position, or a health/condition channel if one is wired). Then item 3 below, the fuse, as the clock's half of the same fix, and run the round with both. The fuse alone is round 10 of asking the prose to do a mechanism's job. The earlier recommendation in this memo was to stop after round 9; that was scoring the experiment rather than the defect, and a threat that arrives and does nothing is a defect worth fixing whatever the round count says.

- Re-entry condition (what flips this to ship): a round on the same two cells where the last-block Momentum is at or above the first treated block in BOTH cells and each session's resolutions name an outcome. Round 8 got one cell there; round 9 got neither. Item 2 is built; the next round runs item 3 (the fuse) together with #1882, not on its own. The structural changes, in order:
  1. DONE (round 8, `7af4d573`): reconciliation on OPEN. An opened entry may carry `covers`; the store refines that thread instead of opening a new one; the extractor sees OVERDUE and DUE NOW marks. Harrowgate's nine threads became four.
  2. DONE (round 9, `06dcf071`): a fired state. A thread whose event has landed (its actor has arrived, its news has been delivered, the thing came through) is marked fired in the ledger; the block renders it as already in the room and stops asking for the arrival, and the pick moves off it (a landing recorded as an advance on the DUE NOW thread clears the pick for the next segment, or the extractor is asked to re-file the thread as the next event now owed). Round 8's Harrowgate t18-29 is the exact case: Henderson in the room since t12, his ledger on the table from t21, "arrives to collect" DUE NOW until t29. Pair it with a check the harness can run: count the judge's "delivered as news again" instances per session and require zero for a ship, and count opens per session so under-filing shows up as a number.
  3. NEXT, and only alongside #1882: a fuse on fired threads. On firing, re-file `dueByTurn` to `firedAtTurn + 3`; when a fired thread is the DUE NOW pick, the section demands the strike, the cost, or the outcome (not the arrival), and `resolved` is the expected exit. Render the thread's summary in the IN THE SCENE mark, not `lastMove`. Declared first, same cells, round 8 as the comparator.
  Then the same two cells again, and if Crystal Lake ends early again, a second attempt is part of the round, not optional.
- Not a retire: the direction is right and cheap (no new call, ~195 tokens while a pick exists), and round 7's Harrowgate vote and Crystal Lake masked man are the two cleanest deadline payoffs the campaign has recorded.
