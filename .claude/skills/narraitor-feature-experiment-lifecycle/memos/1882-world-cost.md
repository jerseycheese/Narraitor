## Decision

- **HOLD** (round 10, 2026-08-19), by the declared rule: G-F passes in both cells and G-G fails in both. PR #1883 stays open with the keying fix on it; the flag default stays off.
- What the round shows: the channel works as a mechanism. Every flag-on turn carried the WORLD COST section and came back with a stamp (30/30 and 28/30, the two misses being turns with no JSON block at all); conditions land on the character and the scene carries them without being told (the judge on Crystal Lake's t18 shoulder: "the injured arm persists for the rest of the transcript - a real mark"; on Harrowgate's last block: "the player ends with less than they started the block with"); a landed thread's strike is attributed to the thread (Davies at t11, Albright at t21, the pursuer at t18). The round-9 baseline of nothing the world could take is gone: 17 and 16 world-took turns.
- Failing gate, G-G: the extractor's idea of a condition is too wide. Three shapes make up almost all of the spam: feelings and transient sensations filed as lasting states ("time", "cold dread", "a sharp weight of urgency and obligation regarding the Rowan parcel", "profound, cold certainty of loss"), one event fanned out into a symptom list (the Harrowgate package: eight conditions in two turns; one Crystal Lake blow: four), and re-imposition under new wording ("discredited before the council" three times in three phrasings). Crystal Lake's extractor cleared 17 of its own 30 (it consolidated at t25, t30, t31); Harrowgate cleared 2 and ended carrying 20. The scene prompt then renders all of them every turn, which is both a token cost and a tone cost.
- G-H's named failure mode, and why it outranks G-G: "suffocating void (death)" and "death" were imposed as conditions on turns where the prose killed the character, and the next segment walked the death back, because nothing in the app ends a session on a narrative death (the #925 finding, memory `project_narrative_fatal_detection`: the live fatal path is the critical-decision failure only). Before this PR a world that could not take could not kill; with the channel and #1872's fuse on, it kills in 16 treated turns and the story does not notice. That is a follow-up issue, not this PR's scope, and it is the first thing to fix before the flag flips.
- What the aborted first run taught, kept on record: the three WORLD_COST unit tests passed `characterId: 'char-1'` by hand and were green while the live channel was silent for 21 turns. The fix added a test at the store layer that proves `addSegment` hands the extraction the session's character (memory `feedback_test_the_layer_that_populates`).

## Re-entry condition (what flips this to ship)
1. Extractor wording, declared first: a condition is a lasting state the character will still have next scene, named as what is wrong with them, never a feeling, a sensation, a sound they made, a pressure they are under, or an event that happened to them; at most one new condition per turn unless the prose plainly states two different lasting injuries; never re-impose a listed condition under new wording, worsening is CLEAR the old plus IMPOSE the new; death, dying and unconsciousness are never conditions.
2. A narrative death or incapacitation ends the session: the extractor (or the scene metadata) flags it in a field the ending path already reads, and the session ends with the epilogue the app already generates for the critical-failure path. Filed against #925's gap as a follow-up to #1882 and #1872; it lands before or with the flag flip.
3. The same two cells, in the same round as the #1872 memo's round-11 fixes (the strike section in the world's register, no clock text in the prose). Ship when G-F holds, G-G's count is at or below 3 per block in both cells, and no condition the prose contradicts within 3 turns appears in either transcript.
