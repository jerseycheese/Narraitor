01. System

Design Philosophy

THE CONCEPT

A fusion of long-form digital journalism and architectural drafting. The app is the "Drafting Table," and the story is the "Manuscript."

THE GOAL

A "blank canvas" that feels premium and intentional. It doesn't compete with the AI's genre; it frames it.

NEUTRALITY

Use a "Paper & Ink" palette with a single functional accent color for interactions.

Narrative Reading Surface Validation

Tested at realistic lengths: short paragraph, full-page segment, and multi-paragraph scene progression.

SHORT PARAGRAPH

The brass lock clicked once, then twice, and the archive doors gave way just enough for candlelight to leak through the seam.

FULL-PAGE SEGMENT

You stepped into the chamber and felt the temperature drop. Shelves climbed three stories high, each packed with ledgers stitched in faded cloth. Above, suspended rails traced the ceiling like constellations of iron, and every few seconds a distant mechanism clattered, then fell quiet again.

A metal desk stood in the center under a cone of light. Its surface was scarred with compass marks and ink circles, and a copper plaque on the edge read: "Records of Irrecoverable Histories." Beneath the desk, a low drawer pulsed with a soft blue glow that brightened when you moved closer.

When you pulled it open, you found a catalog of names scratched into thin brass sheets. Some had been crossed out with decisive strokes; others had notes beside them in hurried script: "relocated," "sealed," "missing witness." At the bottom of the stack sat a single page of vellum, untouched by dust, bearing your own name in fresh black ink.

MULTI-PARAGRAPH SCENE FLOW

You turned the page and the room answered. The rails overhead began to move in sequence, carrying lanterns into a slow orbit around the desk.

Each pass of light revealed another detail: a hidden map etched into the floorboards, a keyhole set into the spine of a bound atlas, and a thread of red string leading from your chair to the locked west door.

When the orbit stopped, the lantern nearest you dipped on its own and projected a final line onto the wall: "Begin where your story was first rewritten."
