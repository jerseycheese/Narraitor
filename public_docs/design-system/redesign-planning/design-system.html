01. System

Design Philosophy

THE CONCEPT

A fusion of long-form digital journalism and architectural drafting. The app is the "Drafting Table," and the story is the "Manuscript."

THE GOAL

A "blank canvas" that feels premium and intentional. It doesn't compete with the AI's genre; it frames it.

NEUTRALITY

Use a "Paper & Ink" palette with a single functional accent color for interactions.

Narrative Reading Surface Validation

SHORT PARAGRAPH

The brass lock clicked once, then twice, and the archive doors gave way just enough for candlelight to leak through the seam.

FULL-PAGE SEGMENT

You stepped into the chamber and felt the temperature drop. Shelves climbed three stories high, each packed with ledgers stitched in faded cloth. Above, suspended rails traced the ceiling like constellations of iron, and every few seconds a distant mechanism clattered, then fell quiet again.

A metal desk stood in the center under a cone of light. Its surface was scarred with compass marks and ink circles, and a copper plaque on the edge read: "Records of Irrecoverable Histories." Beneath the desk, a low drawer pulsed with a soft blue glow that brightened when you moved closer.

When you pulled it open, you found a catalog of names scratched into thin brass sheets. Some had been crossed out with decisive strokes; others had notes beside them in hurried script: "relocated," "sealed," "missing witness." At the bottom of the stack sat a single page of vellum, untouched by dust, bearing your own name in fresh black ink.

MULTI-PARAGRAPH SCENE FLOW

You turned the page and the room answered. The rails overhead began to move in sequence, carrying lanterns into a slow orbit around the desk.

Each pass of light revealed another detail: a hidden map etched into the floorboards, a keyhole set into the spine of a bound atlas, and a thread of red string leading from your chair to the locked west door.

When the orbit stopped, the lantern nearest you dipped on its own and projected a final line onto the wall: "Begin where your story was first rewritten."

Streaming Stability Prototype

Validating buffered rendering, scroll anchoring, and layout stability for AI-generated content. Target: Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) < 0.10.

Lower intervals increase main-thread thrash and risk higher CLS. 75ms is the recommended baseline for high-stability streaming.

Stability Metrics

Cumulative Layout Shift
0.0000
PASS: < 0.10

Validation Checklist

  • □ Single-segment CLS remains < 0.10
  • □ Multi-segment history remains < 0.10
  • □ Scroll anchoring honors manual scroll-up
  • □ No visible "jumping" during choice reveal

Safari Compatibility Note

Safari does not natively support overflow-anchor. This prototype validates that ResizeObserver based anchoring provides an equivalent stable experience for iOS and macOS users without layout thrashing.

Manual Validation Guide

DevTools Benchmark
  1. Open Chrome DevTools -> Performance tab.
  2. Click Record.
  3. Run Single Segment Stream and Multi-Segment History.
  4. Stop recording and check the Experience row for Layout Shifts.
  5. Verify final CLS in the summary matches the in-page counter below 0.10.
UX Stability Check
  1. Start a stream, then manually scroll up before it finishes.
  2. Confirm the viewport stops following new text (anchoring disabled).
  3. Scroll back to the bottom; confirm anchoring re-engages.
  4. Watch the choice reveal; confirm no other elements on the page move.

Migration Handoff (#1033)

Validated Defaults

  • Buffer Interval: 75ms (Optimal balance of perceived speed vs stability)
  • Method: insertAdjacentText (Minimizes DOM thrash)
  • Anchoring: ResizeObserver + Pin State (Safari compatible)

Production Implementation Notes

  • Replace scroll-snap with standard overflow if snap causes jumping during stream.
  • Ensure choices container uses min-height or grid-template-rows to reserve space.
  • Use PerformanceObserver in dev/preview builds to monitor regressions.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

All components and patterns must meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards minimum.

Color Contrast Requirements

STANDARD TEXT (< 18px)
4.5:1 minimum
  • Light mode: text-primary on canvas = 18.27:1 ✓
  • Dark mode: text-primary on dark-canvas = 19.06:1 ✓
LARGE TEXT (≥ 18px or bold ≥ 14px)
3:1 minimum
  • Narrative text (18px) meets enhanced 4.5:1 standard ✓
  • System labels (14px mono, muted) on canvas = 4.68:1 ✓
UI COMPONENTS & GRAPHICS
3:1 minimum
  • Primary accent indicators on canvas = 11.05:1 ✓

Focus Indicators

All interactive elements must have visible focus indicators with 3:1 contrast.

outline: 2px solid var(--color-accent); outline-offset: 2px;

FOCUS STANDARDS

  • Minimum 2px outline or border
  • 2px offset for better visibility
  • Never remove focus styles (no outline: none)
  • Focus order follows logical reading flow
  • Skip links for keyboard navigation

Screen Reader Support

SEMANTIC HTML
  • Use semantic elements (<nav>, <main>, <article>, <section>)
